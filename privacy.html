Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

ZAVLO Technologies ("ZAVLO", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of individuals and organizations interacting with our digital interfaces and connected healthcare infrastructure systems.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly submitted by you through our official inquiry and contact forms, including:

  • Full name and institutional / organizational affiliation
  • Official business email address and telephone contact number
  • Inquiry category (Deployment, Partnership, Technology, or General) and specific message contents
  • Standard, non-identifying technical server logs (such as browser user-agent and viewport dimensions) required for secure website operation

3. Use of Information

Information provided to ZAVLO Technologies is used exclusively for:

  • Evaluating and responding to regional infrastructure, healthcare system, and municipal deployment inquiries
  • Facilitating technical support and partnership dialogues
  • Ensuring the operational integrity and security of our digital systems

4. Data Protection & Security

We apply robust administrative, cryptographic (SSL/TLS), and technical protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of submitted communications. We do not sell, rent, or trade inquiry information to third parties.
